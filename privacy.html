2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Name, email, phone / WhatsApp number
  • Business name, service interest, budget range, and message content from our contact form
  • Any details you send by email, WhatsApp, or social media DMs

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device type, and pages visited (if analytics are enabled)

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data (e.g. health, biometric, or government ID numbers) through this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and book strategy calls
  • To prepare proposals and deliver services you request
  • To send transactional messages related to your request (not spam marketing lists without consent)
  • To improve our website and client experience
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers that help us run the site or receive form submissions (for example FormSubmit or similar email relay services)

Messages you choose to open in WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp’s own privacy terms.

5. Cookies and storage

We may use essential browser storage for site function (e.g. saving a local copy of your form submission). If we add analytics or advertising cookies later, we will update this policy and, where required, request consent.

6. Data retention

Enquiry data is kept only as long as needed to handle your request and maintain ordinary business records (typically up to 24 months for inactive leads, unless a longer period is needed for an active client relationship or legal reasons). Browser localStorage data remains on your device until you clear site data.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure; please avoid sending highly sensitive information via the public contact form.

8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, or object to certain processing. Contact us at motechy123@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children’s privacy

Our services are aimed at businesses and adult founders. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

10. Third-party links

Our site links to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, WhatsApp, and other platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the revised policy.
