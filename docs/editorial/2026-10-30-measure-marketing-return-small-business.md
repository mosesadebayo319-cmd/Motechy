## Original worked example and limitations

Hypothetical Enugu office caterer; every amount is an invented teaching assumption, explicitly labelled. It is not a client result, price quotation, typical budget or market statistic.

- Attributed net sales: ₦600,000; variable fulfilment: ₦360,000.
- Contribution: ₦240,000; contribution ratio: 0.40.
- Marketing: ₦100,000 media + ₦30,000 content + ₦50,000 management/staff = ₦180,000.
- Contribution after marketing: ₦60,000, before remaining overhead and tax.
- Estimated contribution-based marketing ROI: 60,000 / 180,000 × 100 = 33.3% rounded.
- Revenue ROAS: 600,000 / 100,000 = 6×, assuming all those sales are credited to paid advertising under the stated rule.
- Marketing-cost coverage: 180,000 / 0.40 = ₦450,000 revenue; equivalent 4.5× ROAS only for that cost structure.
- Sensitivity: assumed 75% incremental sales with proportional variable costs gives ₦450,000 − ₦270,000 − ₦180,000 = ₦0. The 75% is not estimated from data, and no causal finding is claimed.

The worksheet keeps observed repeat purchases separate from forecasts, treats missing costs as missing, and excludes undefined zero-denominator ratios. Supporting visual repeats the same four arithmetic stages with semantic HTML text. No performance chart, ranking guarantee or AI-detector promise is included.
