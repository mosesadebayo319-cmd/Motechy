# Insights keyword and editorial research

Researched 12 October 2026. Audience: Nigerian business owners buying marketing, websites or business software, with Abuja relevant to MoTechy's location.

## Evidence and limits

These are five editorial priorities, **not a verified ranking of the five most searched keywords**. Selection combines relevance to MoTechy's actual services, live search-result intent and Google autocomplete suggestions. No connected Keyword Planner, Search Console, Ahrefs or Semrush dataset supplied monthly volumes or difficulty scores. None are invented here. A Google Trends request returned HTTP 429; no Trends comparison informs this plan.

Google autocomplete was requested with `hl=en&gl=ng` at `https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&q=QUERY&hl=en&gl=ng`. Suggestions indicate candidate phrases, not search counts or exclusively Nigerian demand. Some results included foreign locations; those were not adopted. Search results contained relevant service, pricing and explanatory pages, supporting the intent choices below but not proving relative popularity.

## Sources and the claims they support

- [Google Keyword Planner metrics](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en): monthly search averages depend on location, period, network and close variants. Obtain a Nigeria-targeted export before making volume comparisons.
- [Google SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide): search fundamentals and clear, discoverable content.
- [Google helpful content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) and [generative AI guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content): useful, accurate, original content is the objective. AI-detector scores are not a ranking target; neither rankings nor detector outcomes are guaranteed.
- [Google local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en), [Business Profile eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en) and [review guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122?hl=en): local relevance, legitimate eligibility and review practices.
- [Search Console performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en), [campaign URL parameters](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10917952?hl=en) and [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): measurement definitions and technical targets, not a promise of business results.
- [WhatsApp Business](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/business-app/): Business App capabilities; no claim that every audience prefers it.
- [Alpha Digital Network's published offer](https://www.alphadigitalnetwork.net/blog/website-design-cost-nigeria/): its own ₦350,000 five-page and ₦650,000 10–15-page offers, checked 12 October 2026. Other market-wide assertions on the source were not adopted.
- [Etchpoint's published offer](https://etchpoint.com/what-a-website-costs/): its own ₦450,000 website starting point, checked 12 October 2026. Two providers do not constitute a national pricing survey.
- MoTechy's `src/data.mjs` and `/services#packages`: the studio's actual Starter/Growth/Scale scopes. Software is quoted by project; invented software package prices are prohibited.
- [W3C forms guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/), [Paystack transaction verification](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/verify-payments/) and [OWASP ASVS](https://owasp.org/projects/asvs): accessible forms, payment verification and application security requirements.
- [Google Article structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article): article metadata that describes the visible page; eligibility is not a guarantee of enhanced search appearance.
