Use this worksheet before comparing proposals. It does not calculate a price or
recommend a custom build automatically. Copy it into your own document and use
sample records rather than private customer information.

## 2. Choose a starting scope

- [ ] Public information and enquiries: assess a company website.
- [ ] A common task such as appointments or selling: trial a suitable existing product.
- [ ] Private records, approval rules or a distinctive workflow: assess application requirements.
- [ ] A focused calculator or learning activity: check whether browser-only behaviour is sufficient.
- [ ] A mixture: separate the public pages from the operational workflow in the brief.

## 4. Records and responsibilities

Repeat for each record type, such as a booking or document:

- Record type:
- Who may create it?
- Who may view or change it?
- Who may approve or cancel it?
- Where is the authoritative copy stored?
- Must it work across devices or staff members?
- How is it exported, retained and eventually deleted?

Keep account ownership and permissions distinct. A successful login alone does
not establish permission to view or change a particular record.
Reference: https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Authorization_Cheat_Sheet.html
