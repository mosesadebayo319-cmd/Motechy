## Before setup or a major change

- [ ] Check the current eligibility rules against actual customer contact.
- [ ] Resolve the existing listing's ownership/access before creating another.
- [ ] Decide whether the operation is a storefront, service-area or hybrid business.
- [ ] Confirm which location details should be public.
- [ ] Agree accurate categories, hours, phone number and website details.
- [ ] Check the details against the website and staff booking instructions.
- [ ] Keep primary ownership under business control; grant suitable team access.
- [ ] Read the requirements for the verification method actually offered.
- [ ] Prepare the requested evidence without exposing sensitive information.
- [ ] After verification, inspect the public profile on a phone.

## Monthly review

- [ ] Read the profile as a customer would; note unexpected or outdated details.
- [ ] Confirm upcoming holiday/closure arrangements with the person responsible.
- [ ] Check service coverage and how appointments are handled.
- [ ] Follow the website link on a phone and inspect the enquiry route.
- [ ] Review recent photos for accuracy and privacy; plan any needed replacement.
- [ ] Read recent reviews and reply where useful without revealing private details.
- [ ] Check whether staff changes require an access review.
- [ ] Record equivalent reporting periods and the metrics available to this profile.
- [ ] Compare platform activity with separately recorded enquiry outcomes.
- [ ] Assign one improvement, an owner and a due date.
