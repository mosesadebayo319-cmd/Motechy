# Social media scope checklist

Prepared by MoTechy. Use this to compare proposals, then agree the final scope in writing.

- Business goal and audience:
- Current account links:
- Platforms and account owner:
- Provider's role/access and multi-factor authentication:
- Planning and approval process:
- Monthly formats and quantities (define a post, carousel and video):
- Who supplies photographs, footage, product details and evidence:
- Filming, travel, editing, captions and usage rights:
- Revision rounds and feedback deadlines:
- Publishing responsibility:
- Community coverage hours, inboxes and escalation rules:
- Advertising management fee and separate media budget:
- Reporting measures, definitions and review meeting:
- Management fee, production costs, tools and applicable charges:
- Contract length, payment terms, cancellation and notice:
- Asset handover, outstanding work and access removal:

Do not treat follower counts or views as verified sales. Define the business outcome and how it will be measured.
