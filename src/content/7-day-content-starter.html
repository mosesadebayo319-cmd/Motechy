How to use: Pick your main platform. Post once per day for 7 days using the prompt. Track DMs, saves, and profile visits — not only likes.

Before day 1

Write one sentence: who you help + the problem you solve. Every post this week should make that clearer.

1

Day 1 — Educate

Answer one question your ideal client asks before they buy. Be specific to your industry.

2

Day 2 — Prove

Share a small result, lesson, process, or before/after (with permission if client work).

3

Day 3 — Engage

Ask one sharp question. Reply to every comment within 24 hours.

4

Day 4 — Humanise

Behind the scenes: you, the craft, a real founder moment — build trust.

5

Day 5 — Educate

A simple 3-step framework. Make it screenshot-worthy.

6

Day 6 — Engage

Contrarian take: “Most people get X wrong. Here’s what works.”

7

Day 7 — Convert

Soft CTA: who you help, how to start, clear WhatsApp or call link.

For every 1 promotional post, publish at least 4 value posts. That’s the MoTechy ratio rule.

End-of-week review (10 minutes)

  • Which post created the best conversations?
  • Which topic felt easiest to talk about?
  • What will you repeat next week?
  • What will you stop doing?
